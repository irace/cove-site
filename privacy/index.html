Privacy

Cove is self-hosted, extensible software. The project does not operate a hosted Cove service or require a central Cove account.

This website

This public website provides information about Cove. It does not offer account creation, accept form submissions, set advertising cookies, or include third-party analytics.

Ordinary infrastructure logs may temporarily record information such as an IP address, request path, browser type, and timestamp for security and reliability.

Your Cove deployment

A Cove owner installs and operates their own deployment. Product URLs, notes, cached images, account credentials, integration tokens, and backups are stored in infrastructure controlled by that owner. The Cove project does not receive a copy.

The operator of a deployment is responsible for securing it, choosing who can access it, and following any privacy laws that apply to their use.

Connected services

Cove can connect to services that an owner explicitly configures. Those services are optional and their own terms and privacy policies apply.

  • Pinterest access is read-only and limited to the boards and Pins needed for a user-directed import workflow. Cove shows saved Pins for review and imports only the items the user chooses.
  • Cove stores the Pinterest access token inside the owner's deployment. Disconnecting removes the local connection. Imported Cove items can be deleted by the owner.
  • changedetection.io may receive product URLs selected by the owner when they enable page monitoring.

Data choices

Because Cove is self-hosted, access, correction, export, deletion, retention, and backup choices are controlled by the deployment owner. Cove includes tools for deleting saved items and disconnecting supported account-level integrations.

Questions

Questions about data in a particular Cove deployment should be directed to that deployment's operator. Questions about this policy can be directed to the project contact supplied with access to Cove.
