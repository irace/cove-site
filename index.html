Research before you buy

A quiet place for every “maybe.”

Cove turns scattered tabs and half-remembered links into a useful, structured catalog. It is designed for deciding, not scrolling.

01

Capture from anywhere

Save a product with the browser extension, bookmarklet, API, or share flow. Cove keeps the source and a local copy of its useful metadata.

02

Organize your way

Use boards, sections, item types, and custom fields to model the way you actually compare furniture, books, gear, gifts, and more.

03

Notice what changed

Track price and availability through optional integrations, while keeping Cove itself focused, fast, and free of recommendation feeds.

Make the comparison yours

Keep the facts you care about.

A camera and a sofa should not have the same fields. Cove supports reusable item types, custom attributes, sortable tables, and direct links back to every original source.

Built for ownership

Your catalog should work for you.

No attention economy

No ads, social graph, promoted products, engagement loops, or algorithmic feed. Cove opens to your own library.

Self-hosted by design

Your deployment holds your catalog, cached media, credentials, and backups. There is no required Cove cloud account.

Adaptable by design

The API is first-class, schemas are flexible, and integrations can add context without taking over the core experience.

Fast on purpose

Search, keyboard navigation, focused views, and compact controls make a personal catalog feel immediate rather than ceremonial.

Connect what you use

Useful at the edges. Independent at the center.

Cove starts with URLs and grows through small, explicit integrations. Each connection is configured by the owner and can be removed without changing the rest of the catalog.
